Project 1 / UK retail · 60 stores · 2023–2024

Sunrise Coffee Chain Analytics

A Power BI report evaluating the commercial performance of a 60-store UK coffee chain. It covers store finances, daily trading and revenue mix, loyalty programme impact, and the regions that deserve expansion capital, then turns them into recommendations. The goal: show where to focus expansion and whether the loyalty programme is worth more investment.

  1. OverviewFinancial and commercial performance
  2. Loyalty ImpactAdoption, revenue and ROI
  3. Revenue MixDaily trading and product mix
  4. Strategic FocusRanked priorities and actions

Headline findings

  • London leads on revenue (£5.09M) but keeps only 7.38% as net profit, the lowest of all 12 regions.
  • Loyalty members generate 33.19% of revenue while redemption costs are just 2.38% of total cost.
  • Recommendation: keep the loyalty programme and measure incremental spend; shortlist regions on composite score before committing capital.

Project 2 / Global labour market · 2021–2024

AI Workforce Transformation

A story of how AI adoption accelerated and reshaped the workforce across countries, industries and skills. Each page answers the next executive question, and the final page turns the evidence into an action plan that adapts to the selected sector, industry or skill.

  1. OverviewScale and pace of change
  2. DriversAutomation exposure by industry and skill
  3. ImpactNet jobs, wages, reskilling
  4. ActionRecommendation that adapts to the selection

Headline findings

  • Manual Skilled Trades is the most exposed skill (replaceability score 79.60) and the largest net loser at −60.5K jobs.
  • Seven industries pair above-average automation susceptibility with above-average adoption, and they account for 25.9% of job displacement.
  • Recommendation: prioritise redeployment, internal mobility and strategic reskilling before expanding AI automation.

Project 3 / Fintech · Kenya, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa · 2024

African Gig Economy and Digital Wallet Risk

Built around one executive decision: where is risk concentrated, why is it happening, and where should intervention come first. A weighted Vulnerability Index (fraud rate, fraud loss, dispute rate, reversal rate) ranks markets, channels and behavioural segments, and each priority is linked to an action and an expected outcome.

  1. PerformanceValue, success, fraud, disputes
  2. Risk concentrationVulnerability Index by entity
  3. DriversProcessing time and velocity score
  4. InterventionAction and expected outcome

Headline findings

  • Ghana leads transaction value (25.5%), but Kenya tops the market vulnerability ranking at 77.77, so it is the first priority.
  • Agent and POS carry 50.0% of transaction value, while the API/third-party channel scores 76.84 on vulnerability.
  • Unverified Tier 0 users, the web channel and bank-transfer wallet funding are rated Critical, with scores between 83.5 and 85.0.

Project 4 / Enterprise sales · 2025

B2B Sales Performance Analysis

A decision-ready view of sales performance for stakeholders who monitor results, compare actuals with targets, find profitable products and customer segments, and understand what sits behind changes in revenue, margin and returns. It follows one path: Performance, Target, Contribution, Drivers, Evidence, Attention.

  1. PerformanceSales, profit, margin, target
  2. ContributionProducts and customers
  3. DriversTeams, channels, promotions
  4. AttentionWhere to look first

Headline findings

  • Sales grew 14.13% to $6.72M, yet only 3 of 12 months beat target. North America carries the largest gap at −$261.17K.
  • Gross margin slipped 1.92 pp to 46.14% despite growth, and 6 products combine above-average returns with below-average margin.
  • Power User Training is the top product at $1.37M, up 127.76% year on year.
